About Top Spin

Professional-grade tables meet a relaxed lounge atmosphere at Southampton's destination for cue sports.

Our story

Top Spin Pool and Sports Bar was founded to raise the bar for Southampton's cue sports scene. We provide a great environment for serious competitors and social players alike, with quality in everything from the tables to the drinks.

You'll find us on the first floor at 88–90 Lodge Road, with easy access from Lodge Road.
