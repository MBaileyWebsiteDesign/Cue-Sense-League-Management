Leagues & tournaments

Compete, improve your game and join Southampton's most dedicated English pool community.

In-house league

Top Spin Singles League

The Singles League is our flagship open competition, with five divisions from the Premier League down to Division 5. Players of every standard — from seasoned pros to improving amateurs — compete against others at a similar level.

Fixtures, tables and results are run through Cue Sense. Top Spin members also get access to the Cue Sense app to manage, monitor and improve their game.

Formats

Find the format that fits your game

We run a mix of weekly, monthly and seasonal events. All tournaments are currently announced through our Facebook group.

Weekly open tournaments

Single-night knockouts open to all levels, drawn on the night, with cash prizes and bar tabs up for grabs.

Seasonal leagues

Structured leagues over several weeks. Earn points each match, climb the table and qualify for end-of-season finals.

Handicap & beginners nights

Newer to competitive play? Handicaps level the field so everyone has a real shot at winning.

Specials & team nights

Doubles, team events and one-off specials with themed nights, added prize money and sponsor giveaways.
