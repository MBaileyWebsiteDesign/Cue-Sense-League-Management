Frequently asked questions

Where is Top Spin?

We're on the first floor at 88–90 Lodge Road, Southampton SO14 6RF. Get directions.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday 11:00 until midnight, and Sunday 11:00 to 22:00.

How much does a pool table cost?

Standard tables are £10 an hour (or £18 for 2 hours) and match tables are £12 an hour. We also offer 1 hour of pool with a 4-pint pitcher for £25.

Do you have Chinese pool tables?

Yes. Alongside our English pool tables we have Chinese pool tables.

How much is membership?

Membership is £40 a month or £400 for 12 months. Payments are currently taken at the bar, so please speak to a member of the team.

What do members get?

Membership gives you access to the Cue Sense app to manage, monitor and improve your game. Find out more at cuesense.co.uk.

Can I pay online?

Not at the moment. Please visit us and pay at the bar.

Is darts free?

Yes. Our six dartboards are free to use and open to everyone. Bring your own darts or borrow a house set from behind the bar.

How do I join a pool league?

The Top Spin Singles League has five divisions, from the Premier League to Division 5, and runs on Cue Sense. For our darts teams, ask at the bar or send us a message.

Where are tournaments announced?

All our tournaments are currently run through our Facebook group.

Do you show live sport?

Yes — Sky Sports, TNT Sports, football, rugby, cricket, the Racing Channel and terrestrial TV on the big screens.

Do you serve food?

Yes. 14" pizzas (£12, ideal for 2), burgers, jumbo hot dogs, chicken and chips, and chips. Food may finish an hour early on league nights or special occasions.

Can I book for a group or team night?

Yes. We can block-book tables and help with custom formats and prizes. Send a group enquiry.

Do you re-tip cues?

Yes. A full re-tip is £25, or £15 if you supply the tip. We also do tip shaping and ball cleaning (£2 a session). Request a slot.

Is there parking?

Yes, there's a car park.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, dogs are allowed.

Are you family friendly?

Yes, Top Spin is family friendly.

What's your phone number?

Our phone line is coming soon. In the meantime, email info@TopSpinPoolAndSportsBar.co.uk or use our contact form.

Can't find your answer? Get in touch.
