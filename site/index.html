The venue

Built for serious English pool

Top Spin Pool and Sports Bar is a destination for cue sports in Southampton. Elite tables meet a social, lounge-style scene, so it works just as well for league players as it does for a relaxed night with friends.

  • Pro-spec English pool tables and quality balls

Darts at Top Spin

Six boards. Free to play.

Top Spin is home to two darts teams, with league matches and practice nights on Mondays and Thursdays.
